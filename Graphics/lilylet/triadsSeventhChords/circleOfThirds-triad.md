# Triads on the circle of thirds

Beethoven, Piano Sonata in C minor, Op. 13 ("Pathétique"), II, opening. The two circled sonorities are shown on the circle of thirds below.

**1 — A triad.** On the circle of thirds C–E–G–B–D–F–A, the notes A, C, E (here A♭, C, E♭) are three adjacent letters: a triad.

**2 — Not a triad.** The notes C, F, D, B (here C, F, D♭, B♭) are not three adjacent letters on the circle, so the sonority is not a triad.
