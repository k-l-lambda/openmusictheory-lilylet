# Progression Patterns Reference

## Classical Progressions

### Basic T-S-D-T

| Pattern | RN | Function |
|---------|-----|----------|
| Standard | I-IV-V-I | T-S-D-T |
| With ii | I-ii-V-I | T-S-D-T |
| With viio | I-IV-viio-I | T-S-D-T |

### Circle of Fifths

| Length | Progression |
|--------|-------------|
| Full | I-IV-viio-iii-vi-ii-V-I |
| Common | vi-ii-V-I |
| Extended | iii-vi-ii-V-I |

### Cadential Approaches

| Cadence | Approach Options |
|---------|------------------|
| PAC | ii-V-I, IV-V-I, ii6-Cad6/4-V-I |
| HC | I-ii-V, I-IV-V, vi-ii-V |
| DC | I-IV-V-vi |

## Blues Progressions

### 12-Bar Blues

```
| I  | I  | I  | I  |
| IV | IV | I  | I  |
| V  | IV | I  | I/V|
```

### Jazz Blues

```
| I7   | IV7  | I7   | I7   |
| IV7  | #IVo7| I7   | vi-ii|
| ii7  | V7   | I7   | V7   |
```

### 16-Bar Blues

```
| I  | I  | I  | I  | I  | I  | I  | I  |
| IV | IV | I  | I  | V  | IV | I  | V  |
```

## Rotation Principle

All 4-chord loops can rotate:

| Original | Rotation 1 | Rotation 2 | Rotation 3 |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| I-vi-IV-V | vi-IV-V-I | IV-V-I-vi | V-I-vi-IV |
| I-V-vi-IV | V-vi-IV-I | vi-IV-I-V | IV-I-V-vi |
| vi-IV-I-V | IV-I-V-vi | I-V-vi-IV | V-vi-IV-I |

## Progression Modifiers

### Adding 7ths

| Basic | With 7ths |
|-------|-----------|
| I-IV-V-I | Imaj7-IVmaj7-V7-Imaj7 |
| ii-V-I | ii7-V7-Imaj7 |
| I-vi-IV-V | Imaj7-vi7-IVmaj7-V7 |

### Using Inversions

| Basic | With Inversions |
|-------|-----------------|
| I-V-vi-IV | I-V/7-vi-IV |
| I-vi-IV-V | I-vi/3-IV-V6/4 |

### Adding Sus Chords

| Position | Usage |
|----------|-------|
| V | Vsus4-V-I |
| I | Isus4-I |
| IV | IVsus2-IV |

## Voice-Leading Optimized

| Progression | Smooth Voice-Leading |
|-------------|---------------------|
| I-vi | Keep common tone (sol) |
| vi-IV | Move bass up 3rd, upper voices down |
| IV-V | Step motion in bass |
| V-I | Leading tone resolution |
| I-V | Common tone (sol) |
| V-vi | ti→do, re→do, sol→mi |

## Avoiding Clichés

| Cliché | Alternatives |
|--------|--------------|
| I-V-vi-IV | I-V-iv-IV, I-V-vi-ii |
| I-vi-IV-V | I-vi-ii-V, I-vi-IV-I |
| i-VII-VI-V | i-VII-iv-V, i-VII-VI-iv |
