# Applied chord D7/V: [S+4] D5 T1

The page's model applied chord, F♯dim → G → C, labeled with two layers of functional bass.

## What to look for

- Bass F♯2 → G2 → C3.
- Lane 1: [S+4] D5 T1. The F♯ chord functions as S in C (raised *fa* = *fi* in the bass, hence +4 and the brackets for a chromatic chord).
- Lane 2, under [S+4]: D7/V, meaning the same chord is a D7 (leading-tone chord with *ti* in the bass) in the key of V (G).
- This is exactly the page's example: "the regular functional bass line would read **[S+4] D5 T1**, and below the **[S+4]** would be the symbol **D7/V**".
