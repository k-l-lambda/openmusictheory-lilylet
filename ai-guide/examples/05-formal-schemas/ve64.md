# Voice exchange with passing V6/4: T(1 D2p 3)

The outer voices exchange do and mi across a passing **V6/4**. It's the first voice-exchange pattern on OMT's keyboard voice-leading schemata page.

## What to look for

- Bass G2 → A2 → B2 (do–re–mi) against soprano B4 → A4 → G4 (mi–re–do). The outer voices swap pitch classes: a **voice exchange**.
- Middle chord: `<D4 F♯4>` + A4 over A2 = D major in second inversion = **V6/4 (D2)**, a passing chord. Figures `6/4` then `6`.
- The inner voices barely move: D4 is held throughout, G4 → F♯4 → G4.
- Label `T(1 D2p 3)`: one tonic prolongation, root position → first inversion, through a passing dominant.
