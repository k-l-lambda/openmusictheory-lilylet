# Voice exchange with passing VII6: T(1 D2p 3)

The same voice exchange as VE64, with a passing **VII6** (figure 6, i.e. 6/3) as the D2 chord.

## What to look for

- Bass G2–A2–B2 against soprano B4–A4–G4: the outer voices exchange do and mi.
- Middle chord: `<C4 F♯4>` + A4 over A2 = F♯ diminished, first inversion = **VII6**. The figure `6` is printed for both the 2nd and 3rd chord.
- The page's point: the *fa* (C4) in the D2 chord moves **up** to D4 instead of resolving down to *mi*. This is allowed because *mi* is already taken by the bass and can't be doubled, and the outer-voice counterpoint is smooth.
- Compare the inner voices with [VE64](ve64.md): only C4 vs D4 differs.
