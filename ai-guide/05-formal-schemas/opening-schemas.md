# Opening Schemas

## Summary

Opening schemas are 4-stage melodic-harmonic patterns used at phrase beginnings, typically as the framework for presentation phrases. All prolong tonic through neighbor or passing motion.

## The Meyer

| Stage | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| **Melody** | do | ti | fa | mi |
| **Bass** | do | re | ti | do |
| **Figure** | 5/3 | 4/3 | 6/5 | 5/3 |
| **RN** | I | V | V | I |
| **Function** | T | D | D | T |

**Character**: Descending then ascending melody, common opening gesture.

## The Jupiter

| Stage | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| **Melody** | do | re | fa | mi |
| **Bass** | do | ti | ti | do |
| **Figure** | 5/3 | 6/5 | 6/5 | 5/3 |
| **RN** | I | V | V | I |
| **Function** | T | D | D | T |

**Variant (root position):**

| Stage | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| **Bass** | do | sol | sol | do |
| **Figure** | 5/3 | 7 | 7 | 5/3 |

**Character**: Named for Mozart's "Jupiter" symphony theme, stepwise ascending melody.

## The Aprile

| Stage | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| **Melody** | do | ti | re | do |
| **Bass** | do | re | ti | do |
| **Figure** | 5/3 | 4/3 | 6/5 | 5/3 |
| **RN** | I | V | V | I |
| **Function** | T | D | D | T |

**Character**: Neighbor motion in melody (do-ti-re-do), circular.

## The Pastorella

| Stage | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| **Melody** | mi | re | fa | mi |
| **Bass** | do | sol | sol | do |
| **Figure** | 5/3 | 7 | 7 | 5/3 |
| **RN** | I | V | V | I |
| **Function** | T | D | D | T |

**Character**: Pastoral, gentle, starts on mi not do.

## The Do-Re-Mi

| Stage | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| **Melody** | do | re | re | mi |
| **Bass** | do | ti | ti | do |
| **Figure** | 5/3 | 6/5 | 6/5 | 5/3 |
| **RN** | I | V | V | I |
| **Function** | T | D | D | T |

**Character**: Simple ascending scale fragment, very common.

## The Sol-Fa-Mi

| Stage | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| **Melody** | sol | fa | fa | mi |
| **Bass** | do | re | ti | do |
| **Figure** | 5/3 | 5/3 | 6/5 | 5/3 |
| **RN** | I | II | V | I |
| **Function** | T | S | D | T |

**Character**: Includes subdominant (II), fuller harmonic cycle.

## The Romanesca

| Stage | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| **Melody** | sol | sol | do | sol |
| **Bass** | do | ti | la | mi |
| **Figure** | 5/3 | 6/3 | 5/3 | 6/3 |
| **RN** | I | V | VI | I |
| **Function** | T | D | Tx | T |

**Character**: Descending bass by thirds, more common in slow movements.

## Compositional Application

### Using Opening Schemas

1. **Presentation phrase**: Use schema for mm. 1-4
2. **Basic idea framework**: Each 2 stages = 1 bar typical
3. **Embellishment**: Add passing tones, rhythmic variety
4. **Repetition**: Schema provides structure for BI + BI

### Schema Selection Guide

| Desired Effect | Schema |
|----------------|--------|
| Strong opening | Meyer, Jupiter |
| Gentle/pastoral | Pastorella |
| Simple/clear | Do-Re-Mi |
| Richer harmony | Sol-Fa-Mi |
| Slow movement | Romanesca |

## Common Features

All opening schemas share:
1. **4 stages** (typically 4 beats or 2 bars)
2. **T-D-D-T** or **T-S-D-T** function
3. **Begin and end on tonic**
4. **Bass moves by step** (mostly)
